Text and phoneme processing
The included JavaScript modules in g2p/ were obtained from the TypeScript sources at the pinned upstream version below.
Madar removed TypeScript annotations and added explicit local ECMAScript module extensions.
The upstream vendor/espeak-ng-ar.mjs is unmodified. Its GNU GPL v3 or later eSpeak NG 1.52.0
binary/data and the ported phonemizer portions are covered by GPL-3.0.txt.
The upstream source references, modification history, build script and wrapper are available at:
https://github.com/Oddadmix/nabra-tts/tree/16b5281ce61993df3526740990cf845a3f3036f4
https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng/tree/1.52.0
Local copies of the wrapper and build script are in this folder.
