The Open World Group converted these ONNX graphs for Transformers.js, including
the merged text encoder and duration predictor (fp16), quantized denoiser (int8)
and waveform decoder (fp16). The packaged graphs are split into binary sections
and reassembled byte for byte in the browser. The original Unicode lookup table
was converted to signed 16-bit integers. No model training or voice cloning was
performed for Madar.
